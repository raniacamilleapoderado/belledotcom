Privacy Policy

Life Maxxing ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website [www.lifemaxxing.com] (the "Site") or purchase our outdoor casual tees and apparel.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us, as well as data collected automatically when you browse or shop on our Site.

A. Information You Provide to Us

We may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Contact & Account Information: Name, email address, phone number, and password if you create an account or submit a message through our contact form.
  • Order & Shipping Details: Billing address, shipping address, apparel sizing preferences, and order history.
  • Payment Information: Payment card details or digital wallet information. (Note: Payments are processed securely through third-party payment processors; we do not store full credit card numbers on our servers.)
  • Communications: Customer support inquiries, product reviews, or newsletter sign-ups.

B. Information Collected Automatically

  • Device & Usage Data: IP address, browser type, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, and time spent on our Site.
  • Cookies & Tracking Technologies: We use cookies and similar technologies to keep items in your shopping cart, remember your preferences, and analyze site traffic.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process, fulfill, and ship your orders for Life Maxxing apparel, as well as manage returns or exchanges.
  • Send transactional communications, including order confirmations, shipping updates, and customer service responses.
  • Improve our website design, product offerings, and customer shopping experience.
  • Send promotional emails about new outdoor tee drops, restocks, or special offers (only if you opt in; you may unsubscribe at any time).
  • Detect, prevent, and address fraud, security issues, or unauthorized transactions.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share your data with trusted service providers necessary to run our business, including:

  • E-Commerce & Hosting Platforms: To power our online store.
  • Payment Processors: Such as Stripe, PayPal, or Shopify Payments to process transactions securely.
  • Shipping & Fulfillment Partners: Carriers (e.g., UPS, FedEx, DHL, or local postal services) to deliver your packages.
  • Analytics & Email Providers: Tools that help us understand website performance and send newsletters you subscribed to.
  • Legal Compliance: When required by law, subpoena, or government request, or to protect our rights and safety.

4. Cookies and Your Choices

Most web browsers accept cookies automatically. You can modify your browser settings to block or delete cookies, though doing so may affect features like saving items in your shopping cart.

  • Marketing Emails: You can opt out of promotional emails at any time by clicking the "Unsubscribe" link at the bottom of any marketing email or contacting us directly.

5. Data Security and Retention

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards—such as SSL/TLS encryption—to protect your personal information. However, no online transmission or storage method is 100% secure. We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill your orders, comply with legal or tax obligations, and resolve disputes.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Delete your personal data, subject to certain legal exceptions, such as tax and transaction records.
  • Opt out of targeted advertising or marketing communications.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

7. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any updates will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
